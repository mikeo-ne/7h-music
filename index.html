Our Story

Built by artists,
for artists.

7H Music Group is a Kampala-born, artist-first music company built on a simple belief: the continent’s most extraordinary talent deserves a home held to the same standard as the music — uncompromising and world-class.

The roster is led by Afro-pop and rap standout Baranga — whose debut album Chapters I Never Wrote and breakout hit Badman have put him on Uganda’s biggest playlists — and Afro-pop rising star Mr Fallback, whose melodic, dance-ready records like Ndi Solidi Mu and Kiwatto are capturing the spirit of East Africa’s new wave. Up next: King Avo, officially set to join the family.

Behind every record is Mike 1ne — the label’s in-house music producer and sound engineer, and an admin of the house. He shapes the 7H sound from the studio, mixing and mastering the releases, running sessions for the roster, and helping steer the business day to day. If it comes out of 7H sounding like that, Mike 1ne is the reason.

Across records, management and publishing, we don’t chase trends. We sign voices that can’t be ignored, and we build careers that outlast the cycle — from the first studio session to the biggest stage.
